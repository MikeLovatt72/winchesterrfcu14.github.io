Your personal development reference · 2026–27

Position Guides

Written directly to you. Find your shirt number.

How to use these

  1. Good is your starting point. If you can do most of it, you are where you need to be right now.
  2. Excellent is your target for this season. Honest self-assessment is the first step.
  3. Outstanding is what you are building towards. It takes time, and that is the point.

Research the players listed on each page. They all have videos on YouTube. Talk to the coaching team about your development, regularly.

The three zones

Every position page talks about three zones of the pitch. Same colours everywhere.

GREENScoring end. Their 22 and around it. Short carries, fast recycling, protect the gain line.
AMBERThe contest. The middle of the pitch. This is where the game is won.
REDDefensive end. Your own 22 and around it. Safe, simple, clear it.
